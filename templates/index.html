System Architecture & Pipeline
Core Project Principle

"AI-based techniques are used mainly for OCR and text processing to extract information from product labels. The final compliance decision is generated by a configurable rule-based compliance engine." This ensures complete transparency, auditability, and adherence to statutory guidelines without black-box halluncinations.

1
Image Preprocessing (OpenCV)

Grayscale conversion, noise filtering (Gaussian Blur), rescaling, and Otsu's adaptive binarization for enhanced contrast.

2
Text Extraction (Tesseract OCR)

Segmenting textual declarations and obtaining word-level confidence metrics with robust Windows path resolution.

3
NLP & Regex Field Extraction

Pattern matching for Product Name, MRP, Net Quantity (units), Manufacturer, Physical Address, and Consumer Care.

4
Legal Metrology Rule Engine

Cross-verifies extracted fields against statutory requirements in SQLite, producing PASS, FAIL, or REVIEW statuses.

5
Audit Report & PDF Generation

Calculates overall compliance score percentage and produces a formal downloadable PDF audit certificate via ReportLab.
