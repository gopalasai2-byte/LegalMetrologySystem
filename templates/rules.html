Verified Official Legal Metrology Sources

The compliance checking engine in this project evaluates declarations exclusively against verified, official statutory publications rather than unverified web scrapes. Each rule in our database carries an explicit source document reference and version identifier for complete audit traceability:

Primary Statute
The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (Act No. 1 of 2010)

Enacted by the Parliament of India to establish and enforce standards of weights and measures, regulate trade and commerce in weights, measures and other goods which are sold or distributed by weight, measure or number.

Key Sections: Section 18 (Declarations on pre-packaged commodities), Section 36 (Penalties for non-compliance).
Central Rules
Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Published under G.S.R. 202(E) and amended periodically (e.g. G.S.R. 779(E)). Prescribes mandatory printed declarations required on every packaged commodity intended for retail sale.

Key Rules: Rule 6 (Mandatory Declarations on package), Rule 12 (Manner in which units shall be expressed).
Notice for Reviewers: Rules in this prototype are configurable and can be extended to cover industry-specific regulations (e.g., electronic items, cosmetics, foodstuffs) through the "Add New Rule" feature without changing the core source code.
